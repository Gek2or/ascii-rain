# v0.12b — authored civic districts

## Scope and continuity

Base: `ASCII_RAIN_Godot_v0_12a_LIVING_CITY.zip`.
This is stage b of the approved Living City plan. Existing Market Ruins and
Transit Spine scenes/shared builder remain byte-identical. Two old generated
clusters (Archive Row, Memory Gardens) are replaced after consuming their original
RNG sequence, preventing unrelated procedural areas from changing randomly.
Only overlapping legacy lamps are omitted inside the newly authored plots.

The setting remains a digitized civilization in roughly 2500–2800. All normal
architecture, synthetic trees, automated equipment and enemies pass through the
unchanged ASCII renderer. Extremely rare physical artifacts retain their isolated
non-ASCII inspection. No gradual conversion of the world into ordinary 3D.

## Geometry and circulation

**Archive Stacks** (old logical ID `archive_row`): authored plot x29..76,
z−44..7. Upper circulation is at +5.5m with two ramps and two bridges. Rear
stepped repositories and recessed drawers give a different silhouette from the
market's stalls/transit's flat platforms. Ground desks and return slots are
placed out of the central through path. Courier rail is high above combat.

**Memory Gardens** (`memory_gardens`): x−74..−25, z18..73. The low terrace has
solid fill, not a fake traversable 2.3m ceiling. Pavilion deck has real headroom
underneath. Two ground ascents reach the terrace; a link reaches the pavilion;
a separate ground ascent supplies an alternative exit. Octagonal canopy and
branching fabricated trees give a less rectilinear skyline. Decorative leaf
segments and branch tips have no costly physics; trunks/planters/railings are solid.
The catch basin is decorative shallow infrastructure, not a swimming or drowning system.

Shared `civic_modules.gd` extends the existing builder with 12-sided cylinders,
16-segment arches, bounded SurfaceTool geometry and sector-based trim. Arch collision
matches the mesh. Declared deck/ramp/terrace floors have supported IDs. Roof props
are deliberately not new walkable surfaces. Critical collision does not disappear
when ambient motion is disabled or when a dynamic visual is culled.

## Encounters, support, navigation

The existing multi-height AStar3D and enemy path follower are preserved. New decks
and ramps enter the graph through the same physical support/clearance tests.
There is no vertical graph shortcut through an overlapping floor slab.

Five new encounter zones: archive_ground/archive_gallery, gardens_ground/
gardens_terrace/gardens_pavilion. Logical district IDs and threat banks are unchanged.
Upper caches/spawns remain on their intended surface; missing upper placement
never falls back to spawning a wave on the street. Authoring and final validation
now share the same minimum distance (12 upper, 18 ground world units).

EncounterDirector performs the downward support query on physics ticks every
0.12s, instead of doing physics queries in render callbacks. Zone checks use the
supported floor, not airborne actor Y. A jump under the pavilion cannot activate
the terrace band; jumping on the lower terrace cannot activate the pavilion band.
Cached support is bypassed after a large position displacement (test teleports/
future spawn relocation). Layer debouncing and cancellation of stale wave requests
are retained. No new enemy archetypes or automatic skill-based difficulty added.

Four caches change locations; the old fourteen-cache total, costs and reward data
remain. Starter cache, visit-any-two condition, teleporter charging, WATCHER and
persistent Reality Archive remain in the same Main scene.

## Motion and audio

`CivicAtmosphere` creates one overhead courier and two reusable AudioStreamPlayer3D
nodes. The garden irrigation pivot is owned by the garden scene. A finite courier
rail has endpoint dwell time. No unpredictable screen-filling particles or new
per-enemy lights. The status board suspends activity on local hostiles and verifies
records for five seconds after threats leave.

Two original 16s PCM16 mono loops at 22050Hz (assets/ambient/), generated by
`tools/build_district_audio.py`. Seeded filtered noise/oscillators/transients,
no external audio samples. Native WAV resource duplicates enable forward looping.
Audio players stop when the player leaves their finite region, ambient volume is
zero, SFX is zero, or the owning scene exits. Local threats lower their gain to
40% before smooth recovery. Nodes inherit SceneTree pause. Existing music stays
unchanged; this is environmental sound, not a replacement music composition.

A separate `ambient_volume` field is persisted in the same settings file with
default 0.55; RENDER_SCHEMA stays 4. Ambient motion and ambient sound are independent.
Setting motion to zero hides the courier/freezes irrigation without removing floors.
Low hides the archive courier. Finite distance limits are not a claim of measured
physical Android frame-time improvement.

## Reproduce validation

`python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_*.py'`
`python tools/static_gdscript_guard.py`
`python tools/validate_project.py`

With a trusted Linux Godot debug template and Xvfb:
`xvfb-run -a python tools/run_debug_template_checks.py --template /path/to/template --render --timeout 240`

Temporary resource serialization/remaps are isolated and never packaged. Native
physics and GDScript execute in Godot, unlike Python source-contract checks. The
source project is still imported normally by a user's editor. Every captured
camera is deliberately positioned to inspect an architectural route. Human combat
balance, ordinary stable editor import and device GPU/touch remain separate tests.

See raw logs and `native_provenance.json`; new checks are not counted again as
separate successful full playthroughs. Camera capture is not proof of navigation;
actual player and enemy ascents have dedicated native assertions.

## Limits and next scope

Largest-normal-enemy conservative capsule remains shared. There is no full crowd
avoidance, moving-obstacle rebake, flying-enemy route model, new WATCHER traversal,
climbing ladders or lift schedule. Archives/Gardens are readable prototype geometry,
not asset-quality claims matching the approved concept art. Underground Utility
and linked rooftops belong to stage c. The city's extent and shader stay unchanged.
