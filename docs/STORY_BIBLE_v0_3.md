# ASCII//RAIN — Story Bible v0.3

## Core premise

The game takes place in a far-future alternative civilization, roughly 2500–2800. Human experience has been fully digitized. The world still has true 3D geometry underneath, but every ordinary citizen perceives it through a mandatory glyph transcoder: architecture, people, weather, weapons, light and motion are rendered as symbols.

The protagonist does not begin as an outsider. To him, this is simply reality.

During repeated survival runs he starts encountering impossible objects that have no digital provenance. They are **physical records** from before total digitization: photographs, paintings, toys, handwritten pages, mechanical objects, pieces of clothing, analog audio and eventually extremely rare people who are not represented by the glyph layer.

## Non-negotiable visual rule

The ASCII world does **not** gradually turn into conventional 3D. The city remains encoded. The contrast comes from extremely rare non-encoded objects and characters. A true 3D object should feel almost shocking precisely because everything around it remains glyph-based.

## Narrative progression

- Early runs: the protagonist treats physical artifacts as corrupted data.
- Mid game: artifacts establish that colors, faces, nature and private memory existed outside the system.
- Later: the protagonist learns that the glyph world is not merely a visual interface but a social control layer that determines what can be perceived, remembered and therefore questioned.
- Late game: rare non-encoded humans challenge the official explanation of why digitization happened.

## Roguelike integration

Story is discovered through play rather than long cutscenes. Each district can contain one rare reality fragment. The fragment enters a persistent archive and survives death/reset. Combat relics are temporary run power; reality fragments are permanent narrative knowledge.

## District 01 fragment

**PHOTOGRAPH // FAMILY IN SUNLIGHT**

The first physical artifact is a framed family photograph. It contains blue sky, green grass and warm sunlight. The system has no glyph index for the source. The protagonist briefly remembers a word he was never taught: **sunlight**.

This is intentionally quiet. It establishes the central mystery without immediately explaining it.
