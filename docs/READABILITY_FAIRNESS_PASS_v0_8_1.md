# ASCII//RAIN v0.8.1 — Readability & Fairness Pass

Based on the first real desktop screenshot of v0.8.

## Fixed from real playtest
- Bottom-left HUD is now viewport-anchored, so health/XP no longer fall outside short embedded windows.
- Reticle is hidden on death/clear screens.
- World/ASCII exposure raised without removing the night-city look.
- Player materials and forward light are more readable.
- First district encounter is delayed and smaller.
- Regular ambient waves are less frequent early in the run.
- Enemies spawn farther away and get a short materialization grace period before pursuing/attacking.
- Fog reduced and mid-tone preservation improved in the ASCII post-process.
