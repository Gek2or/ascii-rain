IMPLEMENTED
- Visual recovery: discrete stable glyph identity, darker between-glyph space,
  bounded strokes, edge-only bar selection, no random alphabet and no full-size
  unfiltered 3D overlay. Existing 4–10px atlases retained.
- Reference Mood / Readable Combat / Mobile Safe styles. One-time render schema-5
  migration backs up old settings; audio, controls, ambience and archive retained.
- Broader dark player silhouette with separate edge/back highlights. Existing
  procedural locomotion/IK and weapon handling remain; not a new skeleton.
- Pilot stepped market frontage on existing wall footprints, warm local accents.
- Utility Depths: actual -6m hall beneath street, two matching render/collision
  cuts and two walkable ramps, pressure machinery and finite-distance ambience.
- Rooftop Relay: three linked +10m roof sections, two independent ascents,
  cover, relay mast and rotating antenna. Existing four districts retained.
- Multi-height A* now includes below-street routes, no unrelated ground spawn
  fallback on basement/roof bands, and street ascent/descent guidance.
- Six per-district enemy compositions using the existing five archetypes.
- Six OPTIONAL console variations of a shared defense activity: warning, two
  waves, synchronization, clear defenders, separate single reward claim.
  Not six unrelated minigames. At least three successful spawns required.
- Leaving/dying/boss transition aborts without reward; pause freezes event/hazards.
  Optional activities do not become six mandatory tasks in the main run.
- Utility's event adds delayed pressure vents in side bays; subterranean line
  warnings now preserve negative Y rather than clamping to street height.
- Old caches redistributed (still 14), original weapons/music/2 story artifacts
  retained. New areas reuse existing atmosphere loops, not a newly composed song.
